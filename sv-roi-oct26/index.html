Questions founders actually ask

The honest FAQ.

Why would I send $3,500 to a brand I don't know?

Don't — not before the call. The strategy session is free and useful either way; judge us there. Then check receipts: the mentor bench on raisable.vc, 50+ events on Luma. Full refund until Kickoff.

Do I still need my own conference tickets?

Yes. This wraps around Tech Week and Disrupt — it doesn't replace them. We help you pick what matters in the Game Plan session.

Will it conflict with conference hours?

No — rooms run at 08:00 and 18:30. About 10 of your ~80 waking hours that week are ours; the rest are yours, with a plan.

What if my US visa is denied?

Documented denial = full refund or free deferral, your choice. The three prep sessions run remotely regardless — the positioning work is yours either way.

How strong will the other founders be?

The bar is public: venture-scale, raising within 6 months, live product, interview required. One company per seat, no direct competitors. We'd rather run with 5 great founders than 8 average ones.

Exactly how many investor intros do I get?

No quota, on purpose — manufactured numbers produce meetings nobody wanted. Guaranteed instead: three named rooms where investors outnumber founders, and intros all week wherever there's real fit. Want a structured pipeline after? That's the 90-Day Sprint.

Can my co-founder join?

Yes — $1,500 flat, full access, capped at 3 per cohort so rooms stay small.

What happens after the week?

A written 90-day plan, witnessed commitments, check-ins at day 30 and 90. Cohort members can join the 90-Day Sprint — pitched once, at the close.

How do I pay from Ukraine / India / Singapore?

Stripe card payment or a proper company invoice from a named US entity. Two installments available. No wire-only checkout — that's what scams look like.
